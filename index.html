Recognition of Prior Learning

Africa's practical skills, made visible

Capture evidence of work, map it to a competency standard, and keep the qualified assessor in control.

ⓘ
This is an application-stage prototype. The analysis uses fixed demonstration data to show the proposed Gemini workflow. It does not issue certificates or make real competency decisions.

The opportunity

Experience is valuable. Proof should be accessible.

Across Africa, many artisans develop strong practical skills through informal apprenticeships and years of work, but lack recognised evidence of those abilities. Proofmark prepares structured video evidence for review under Recognition of Prior Learning processes.

Our principleAI prepares the evidence. Qualified people make the decision.
01

Artisans

Capture guided evidence of practical work using an accessible mobile-first process.

02

03

Receive consistent evidence reports and auditable human decisions for authorised processes.

Business model

For artisans
